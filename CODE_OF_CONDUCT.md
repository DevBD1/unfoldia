# Unfoldia community code of conduct

Unfoldia welcomes people across languages, cultures, backgrounds, abilities,
and levels of experience. Be patient, assume good intent where reasonable,
criticize ideas rather than people, and explain feedback constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation,
and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Respect privacy and do not
publish another person's personal information without consent. These expectations
apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and project community spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict
participation, or ban people according to severity and repetition. They should
explain decisions when safe, protect reporters, and avoid adjudicating complaints
about themselves. Appeals may be requested through a non-involved maintainer
when one is available; an independent review panel does not currently exist.

For a concern, contact a maintainer through an established private contact, if
available. Do not open a public issue with sensitive details. If no safe private
project contact exists, use GitHub's platform abuse-reporting tools. A dedicated
project reporting inbox has not been established; this document does not claim
one exists. Maintainers should establish one before expanding community spaces.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.
