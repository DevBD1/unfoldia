# Unfoldia governance

Unfoldia is an early-stage, maintainer-led open-source project initiated by Burak
(DevBD1). Community-driven means that people can propose, discuss, review, and
contribute improvements; it does not mean open write access or automatic acceptance.

## Responsibilities and decisions

- Contributors propose scoped changes, disclose provenance, and respond to review.
- Reviewers check learning accuracy, language, accessibility, compatibility, and
  evidence. Subject expertise is valuable independently of code authorship.
- Repository maintainers make merge and release decisions. Burak is the initial
  maintainer; additional maintainers must be explicitly appointed by the owner.
- Production publishing, permissions, secrets, and infrastructure remain
  owner-controlled. CI is not authorization to deploy.

Use GitHub issues and pull requests for public proposals and decisions. Discuss
large changes before implementation. A maintainer should record the rationale
when accepting, declining, or deferring a proposal. There is no promised response
time, voting system, or paid support. Disagreements should focus on the product
principles and evidence, with the maintainer responsible for the final decision.

## Credit and rights

The community identity is **Unfoldia contributors**. Preserve existing copyright
and third-party attribution. Contributions do not transfer copyright to the
maintainer; contributors must have the right to submit their work under the
project's existing MIT license. No CLA or new license is introduced here.
See [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md) for recognition and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
for the workflow.
